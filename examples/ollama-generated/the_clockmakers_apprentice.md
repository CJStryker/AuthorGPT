# Chapter 1. The Hour That Vanished

## 1.1 A Gear Out of Place

Elian found the brass gear beneath the master clock at dawn, warm as if it had fallen from a living machine. The tower above him struck six times, then paused before the seventh note could ring. Across the city, bakers forgot their dough, guards forgot their passwords, and one old woman forgot the name of the street where she had lived for fifty years. Master Orro told Elian to put the gear back without questions. That, naturally, was when the questions began.

## 1.2 The Ledger of Lost Minutes

Behind the workshop shelves, Elian discovered a ledger bound in cracked blue leather. Each page listed a date, a time, and a memory collected from the city: a first kiss, a betrayal, a child’s laugh, a soldier’s fear. The clock did not merely measure time. It stored what people could not bear to carry. Elian understood then why Orro guarded the tower so fiercely. If the clock failed, the city would remember everything at once.
